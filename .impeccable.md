## Design Context

### Users
Dual audience: freelance business clients (SME owners in South Africa and beyond who want a site or app built) and tech recruiters / employers evaluating Dylan as a candidate. Both groups arrive skeptical and leave with a yes/no. The portfolio must earn trust from a business owner who doesn't know what React is, and simultaneously signal craft to a senior engineer reviewing a candidate.

### Brand Personality
Warm, crafted, present. Not corporate-cool. Not edgy-dev. Feels like a real person made it — someone who cares about the work and the people the work is for.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined and elevated light theme. Keep the structural bones of the current Apple-inspired clean layout, but push typography, spacing, warmth, and detail much further. The current palette is a cold Apple-clone (#fafafa, #0071e3, #86868b) — the redesign warms everything with terracotta accents and amber-tinted neutrals. Feels like natural light, paper, and craft rather than glass and silicon.

Anti-references: heavy glassmorphism, generic neon-on-dark dev templates, anything that looks like a Tailwind UI starter kit.

### Fonts
- **Display (headings)**: Bricolage Grotesque — geometric-humanist fusion, variable weight 200–800, optical size axis. Has a handmade quality that reads as "crafted by a real person." Warm and distinctive without being eccentric.
- **Body**: Karla — humanist grotesque, warm letterforms, excellent screen readability.

### Color Palette
Warm terracotta accent against a barely-warm off-white canvas.
- Canvas: #F9F8F4 (warm off-white)
- Surface: #F0EDE5 (card/alt surface)
- Ink: #221D15 (warm near-black)
- Ink-2: #726A58 (warm mid gray)
- Ink-3: #9E9787 (muted/tertiary)
- Border: #E2DED6 (warm light border)
- Accent: #B5623D (terracotta — replaces Apple blue)
- Accent-deep: #8E4A2A (hover/pressed)

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth through restraint** — the warmth comes from subtle amber undertones in neutrals, not loud colors. Never cold.
2. **Typography as identity** — Bricolage Grotesque on all headings at high weight creates an unmistakable voice. Tracking reduced vs Inter defaults since Bricolage has better native spacing.
3. **Craft over flash** — the DS monogram watermark in the hero is the kind of detail a human designer adds. Barely visible, purely personal.
4. **Human scale** — every size, spacing, and color decision asks "does this feel like it was made for a person?" not "does this look like a portfolio template?"
5. **No borrowed aesthetics** — nothing that reads as "AI made this in 2024": no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no neon accents, no hero metric layouts.
